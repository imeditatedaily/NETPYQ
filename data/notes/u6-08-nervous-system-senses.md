# Nervous System and Special Senses

> **At a glance** — CNS = **brain + spinal cord** · **31 pairs** of spinal nerves, **12 pairs** of cranial nerves (IV = **trochlear**) · **dendrites carry impulses towards the cell body** · coverings: **endoneurium** (innermost) → perineurium → epineurium · junction of two neurons = **synapse** · hypothalamus is in the **forebrain** · cerebellum = posture; medulla = vital centres; hypothalamus = temperature, hunger, thirst · **parasympathetic** = "rest and digest" (↓ heart rate, ↑ digestive juices); **sympathetic** = fight-or-flight, stress · eye: vascular coat = choroid + iris; rhodopsin = opsin + retinal; 6 eye muscles; 3 cone types · hearing: auricle → tympanic membrane → ossicles → vestibule → cochlea

Syllabus: "Nervous system and glands: neuron, divisions of the nervous system, sensory and motor systems, synapse, reflexes, cerebrospinal fluid, blood–brain barrier." Prāṇāyāma, relaxation and meditation all act through this system, so it carries many questions.

## 1. Neuron and nerve

**Neuron** (तंत्रिका कोशिका) — the structural and functional unit of the nervous system (~86 billion in the brain).
- **Cell body (soma)** with nucleus and **Nissl bodies** (rough ER).
- **Dendrites** — short, branched processes that **receive** signals and carry impulses **towards the cell body**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2018DEC-P2-Q24, YOGA-2024SEP-P2-Q104]]
- **Axon** — single long process carrying impulses **away from the cell body** to the axon terminals; begins at the **axon hillock**.
- **Myelin sheath** — insulating fat layer: made by **Schwann cells** in the PNS and **oligodendrocytes** in the CNS; gaps = **nodes of Ranvier**, where the impulse jumps (**saltatory conduction**: up to ~120 m/s). Multiple sclerosis destroys CNS myelin.

**Types of neuron**: by function — **sensory (afferent)**, **motor (efferent)**, **interneurons** (association, ~90%); by shape — unipolar/pseudounipolar (sensory ganglia), bipolar (retina, olfactory), multipolar (motor neurons).

**Neuroglia** (support cells; more numerous than neurons): astrocytes (blood–brain barrier, support), oligodendrocytes, microglia (immune), ependymal cells (line ventricles, make CSF); in the PNS, Schwann cells and satellite cells.

**Properties of nerve tissue:** **excitability** (irritability: responding to a stimulus) and **conductivity** — the property that carries the impulse from the area of stimulation to other areas. [[PYQ: YOGA-2021JUN-P2-S49]]

**Coverings of a peripheral nerve**: **endoneurium** — the **innermost**, around each nerve fibre [[PYQ: YOGA-2022DEC-P2-Q22]]; **perineurium** — around a bundle (fascicle); **epineurium** — around the whole nerve.

**Synapse** — the **junction between two neurons** (term by Charles Sherrington, 1897). [[PYQ: YOGA-2025JUN-P2-Q076]] At a **chemical synapse**, the impulse releases a **neurotransmitter** from vesicles into the **synaptic cleft**; it binds receptors on the next neuron (excitatory or inhibitory postsynaptic potential). **Electrical synapses** use gap junctions (fast, two-way). Transmission is one-way with a **synaptic delay** of ~0.5 ms.

**Neurotransmitters** [[LIKELY]]: **acetylcholine** (NMJ, parasympathetic, memory), **noradrenaline** (sympathetic), **dopamine** (reward, movement — lost in Parkinson's; excess in schizophrenia), **serotonin** (mood, sleep — low in depression), **GABA** (main **inhibitory**; relaxation — raised after yoga in studies), **glutamate** (main **excitatory**), **endorphins and enkephalins** (natural opioids: pain relief, well-being).

## 2. Divisions; brain and spinal cord

```
Nervous system
├── Central (CNS): brain + spinal cord
└── Peripheral (PNS): 12 pairs cranial + 31 pairs spinal nerves
    ├── Somatic (voluntary: skeletal muscle, skin senses)
    └── Autonomic (involuntary: heart, smooth muscle, glands)
        ├── Sympathetic
        └── Parasympathetic
```

The **two main parts of the CNS are the brain and the spinal cord**; the sympathetic and parasympathetic systems are parts of the peripheral (autonomic) system. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P2-Q25]]

**Brain** (~1.3–1.4 kg; 2% of body weight but ~20% of the body's oxygen):

| Division | Parts | Functions |
|---|---|---|
| **Forebrain** (prosencephalon) | **cerebrum** (telencephalon); **thalamus** and **hypothalamus** (diencephalon) | thought, sensation, voluntary movement; thalamus = sensory relay; hypothalamus = homeostasis |
| **Midbrain** (mesencephalon) | corpora quadrigemina, cerebral peduncles | visual and auditory reflexes |
| **Hindbrain** (rhombencephalon) | **pons**, **medulla oblongata**, **cerebellum** | vital centres, balance |

The **hypothalamus** is part of the **forebrain** (diencephalon). [[PYQ: YOGA-2019DEC-P2-Q086]]

**Functions of the main parts:**

| Part | Functions |
|---|---|
| **Cerebrum** | largest part; two hemispheres joined by the **corpus callosum**; cortex = grey matter. Higher functions: thinking, memory, **emotions** (limbic cortex), **hearing** (temporal lobe), **vision** (occipital lobe), speech, voluntary movement |
| — frontal lobe | **motor cortex** (precentral gyrus), **Broca's area** (speech production, 1861), planning, personality, judgement |
| — parietal lobe | **somatosensory cortex** (postcentral gyrus): touch, pain, temperature, body sense |
| — temporal lobe | **auditory cortex**, **Wernicke's area** (understanding speech, 1874), memory (hippocampus) |
| — occipital lobe | **visual cortex** |
| **Limbic system** (hippocampus, amygdala, cingulate gyrus, hypothalamus) | emotion, memory, motivation |
| **Thalamus** | relay station for all senses except smell |
| **Hypothalamus** | **body temperature**, **hunger and thirst**, sleep–wake and **circadian rhythm** (SCN), emotions and sexual drive, controls the **pituitary** and the **autonomic** system |
| **Cerebellum** ("little brain") | **posture, balance**, coordination of voluntary movement, muscle tone (damage → ataxia) |
| **Pons** | respiratory centres (pneumotaxic, apneustic); relays between cerebrum and cerebellum |
| **Medulla oblongata** | **vital centres**: respiratory, cardiac and vasomotor; reflexes of swallowing, vomiting, coughing, sneezing |

Matching asked: **cerebrum** → controls emotions, hearing and vision; **cerebellum** → posture and postural activities; **medulla oblongata** → the respiratory and cardiovascular centres; **hypothalamus** → body temperature, hunger and thirst. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P3-Q69]]

**Spinal cord** — ~45 cm long, from the medulla to **L1–L2** vertebra, ending as the conus medullaris and the **cauda equina**. Grey matter (butterfly-shaped) inside, white matter (tracts) outside; ascending tracts carry sensation, descending tracts (e.g. corticospinal) carry motor commands.

**Spinal nerves — 31 pairs**: **8 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral, 1 coccygeal**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2018DEC-P2-Q20]] (Note: 7 cervical vertebrae but 8 cervical nerves.) Each has a dorsal (sensory) root and a ventral (motor) root.

**Cranial nerves — 12 pairs**:

| No. | Name | Type | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Olfactory | sensory | smell |
| II | Optic | sensory | vision |
| III | Oculomotor | motor | most eye movements, pupil constriction |
| **IV** | **Trochlear** | motor | superior oblique muscle; **smallest** cranial nerve |
| V | Trigeminal | mixed | face sensation, chewing; **largest** |
| VI | Abducens | motor | lateral rectus |
| VII | Facial | mixed | facial expression, taste (front 2/3 of tongue), tears, saliva |
| VIII | Vestibulocochlear | sensory | hearing and balance |
| IX | Glossopharyngeal | mixed | taste (back 1/3), swallowing, parotid saliva |
| X | **Vagus** | mixed | heart, lungs, gut — **longest**; carries ~75% of parasympathetic fibres |
| XI | Accessory | motor | sternocleidomastoid, trapezius |
| XII | Hypoglossal | motor | tongue movement |

The **fourth cranial nerve is the trochlear**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2019DEC-P2-Q087]]

**Meninges, CSF and blood–brain barrier** [[LIKELY]]:
- **Meninges** (outside → in): **dura mater**, **arachnoid mater**, **pia mater**; CSF flows in the **subarachnoid space**.
- **CSF**: made by the **choroid plexus** in the ventricles (~**500 mL a day**), total volume ~**150 mL**, absorbed by **arachnoid villi** into the venous sinuses. Functions: cushions the brain (buoyancy), removes wastes, keeps the chemical environment stable. Sampled by lumbar puncture (below L2).
- **Blood–brain barrier**: **tight junctions** between capillary endothelial cells, supported by **astrocyte** end-feet; lets through O₂, CO₂, glucose, fat-soluble substances, keeps out most drugs and toxins.

**Reflexes** [[LIKELY]] — a **reflex arc**: receptor → **sensory (afferent) neuron** → integrating centre (spinal cord or brainstem) → **motor (efferent) neuron** → effector (muscle or gland). **Knee jerk** (patellar reflex, L2–L4) is a **monosynaptic stretch reflex**; withdrawal reflex is polysynaptic. Conditioned reflexes (Pavlov) are learned.

**Voluntary control of breathing** in prāṇāyāma comes from the **cerebral cortex** (see *Cardiovascular and Respiratory Systems*).

## 3. The autonomic nervous system

| | **Sympathetic** | **Parasympathetic** |
|---|---|---|
| Outflow | **thoracolumbar** (T1–L2) | **craniosacral** (cranial nerves III, VII, IX, **X**; sacral S2–S4) |
| Ganglia | near the spinal cord (sympathetic chain) | near or in the organs |
| Transmitter at the organ | mostly **noradrenaline**; also adrenaline from the adrenal medulla | **acetylcholine** |
| Response | "**fight or flight**", stress, exercise | "**rest and digest**" |
| Heart | rate and force ↑ | **rate ↓** |
| Pupils | dilate | constrict |
| Airways | dilate | constrict |
| Digestion | inhibited; sphincters close | **digestive juices and motility ↑** |
| Blood sugar | ↑ (glycogenolysis) | — |
| Blood vessels | constrict (skin, gut); dilate in muscle | — |
| Bladder | relaxes | contracts (urination) |
| Salivation | thick, scanty | watery, copious |
| Mind and muscles | alertness, anxiety, muscle tension | calm, **muscle relaxation** |

- The part responsible for "**rest and digest**" is the **parasympathetic** system. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P3-Q19]]
- Parasympathetic stimulation causes **decreased heart rate** and **muscle relaxation** (not more heart rate or anxiety). [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P3-Q40]]
- **Parasympathetic activation increases the secretion of digestive juices.** [[PYQ: YOGA-2025JUN-P2-Q052]]
- Stress causes over-activity of the involuntary organs through the **sympathetic nerves**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2018JUL-P2-Q30]]

**Yoga and the ANS** — slow breathing with long exhalation, bhrāmarī, śavāsana and meditation raise **vagal (parasympathetic) tone** and heart-rate variability; rapid practices such as kapālabhāti and bhastrikā are sympathetic-activating. Right-nostril breathing (*sūrya*) is reported to be sympathetic, left-nostril (*candra*) parasympathetic. [[LIKELY]]

## 4. Special senses: the eye

**Three coats of the eyeball:**
1. **Fibrous** (outer): **sclera** (white, opaque, protective) and **cornea** (transparent, front; main refracting surface).
2. **Vascular** (middle, the **uvea**): **choroid** (blood vessels, pigment), **ciliary body** (makes aqueous humour; ciliary muscle for accommodation), **iris** (coloured; the pupil in its centre).
3. **Nervous** (inner): **retina**.

So the **vascular coat is made of the choroid and iris** (with the ciliary body). [[PYQ: YOGA-2019JUN-P2-Q26]]

**Retina** — photoreceptors:
- **Rods** (~120 million): dim light, black-and-white, night vision; pigment **rhodopsin** (visual purple) = protein **opsin** + **retinal** (from **vitamin A**) — vitamin A deficiency causes night blindness.
- **Cones** (~6–7 million): bright light, **colour** and fine detail; concentrated in the **fovea centralis** (sharpest vision, in the macula). **Three kinds** — sensitive to red, green and blue (Young–Helmholtz trichromatic theory).
- **Optic disc** = **blind spot** (no receptors; optic nerve leaves).

Light-sensitive pigments **are** present in the cones; and different combinations of the three kinds of cones **do** produce the sensation of different colours (so the reason given as "do not" was false). [[PYQ: YOGA-2019JUN-P2-Q24]]

**Eye matching** asked: **rhodopsin → opsin**; **oculomotor muscles → 6**; **image → retina** (real and inverted); **cones → 3** types. [[PYQ: YOGA-2025JUN-P2-Q101]] The **six extra-ocular muscles**: superior, inferior, medial and lateral recti; superior and inferior obliques (nerves: III, IV for superior oblique, VI for lateral rectus). Trāṭaka and eye exercises work these muscles.

**Path of light**: cornea → aqueous humour → pupil → **lens** → vitreous humour → retina → optic nerve → optic chiasma → lateral geniculate body (thalamus) → **occipital** visual cortex. [[LIKELY]] Refractive errors: **myopia** (short sight; concave lens), **hypermetropia** (long sight; convex lens), **presbyopia** (age), astigmatism; **cataract** (lens opacity), **glaucoma** (raised eye pressure).

## 5. Special senses: ear, taste, smell, skin

**Ear** — three parts:
- **External**: **auricle (pinna)**, external auditory canal (wax glands), up to the **tympanic membrane** (eardrum).
- **Middle**: three **ossicles** — **malleus** (hammer), **incus** (anvil), **stapes** (stirrup; smallest bone) — which amplify sound; **Eustachian (auditory) tube** to the pharynx equalises pressure.
- **Inner** (bony labyrinth in the temporal bone): **cochlea** (2¾ turns; **organ of Corti** with hair cells = hearing), **vestibule** (utricle and saccule: linear acceleration, head position) and **three semicircular canals** (rotation). Hearing range ~20–20,000 Hz.

**Order of hearing**: **auricle → tympanic membrane → ossicles → vestibule** (stapes pushes the oval window) → **cochlea** (hair cells turn vibration into nerve impulses → cochlear nerve → auditory cortex, temporal lobe). [[PYQ: YOGA-2025JUN-P2-Q092]]

**Taste** (gustation) — taste buds on the tongue's papillae; five basic tastes: **sweet, sour, salty, bitter, umami**; nerves VII, IX, X. **Smell** (olfaction) — olfactory epithelium in the nasal roof; nerve I; the only sense not relayed through the thalamus first; closely tied to the limbic system (emotion, memory).

**Skin senses** [[LIKELY]]: **Meissner's corpuscles** (light touch), **Merkel discs** (touch, pressure), **Pacinian corpuscles** (deep pressure, vibration), **Ruffini endings** (stretch), **free nerve endings** (pain, temperature). **Proprioceptors**: muscle spindles, Golgi tendon organs, joint receptors — the basis of body awareness in asana.

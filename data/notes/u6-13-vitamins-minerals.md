# Nutrition II: Vitamins, Minerals, Antioxidants and Phytochemicals

> **At a glance** — fat-soluble: **A, D, E, K**; water-soluble: **B-complex, C** · vitamins give **no energy** but regulate metabolism · **B1 thiamine → beriberi** (polyneuritis) · **B2 riboflavin → angular stomatitis** · **B3 niacin → pellagra** (diarrhoea, dermatitis, dementia) · biotin → dermatitis · **B12 cyanocobalamin → pernicious / megaloblastic anaemia** (needed for DNA synthesis in RBCs) · **C → scurvy** (subcutaneous bleeding); richest: **amla > guava > orange > tomato** · **D → rickets** (children; hypocalcaemia) and **osteomalacia** (adults) · K → clotting · provitamin A = **β-carotene** · calcium: **ragi** · magnesium deficiency → **tetany** · copper → skin and hair colour · lycopene–red, β-carotene–orange, betalains–deep red, anthocyanins–blue/purple

Syllabus: "Micronutrients: fat-soluble and water-soluble vitamins, minerals, antioxidants." Vitamin deficiency matching is asked almost every year.

## 1. Vitamins: general facts

**Vitamins** — organic compounds needed in **small amounts** for normal metabolism, which the body cannot make in enough quantity. They **do not supply energy**; they regulate metabolic reactions (mostly as **coenzymes**) and help the body use other nutrients — so, though needed in small amounts, they are important constituents of a balanced diet. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P3-Q48]]

**History**: James **Lind** (1747) cured scurvy in sailors with citrus; Christiaan **Eijkman** (1897) — beriberi from polished rice; Casimir **Funk** (1912) coined "vitamine" (vital amine); E. V. **McCollum** (1913) — fat-soluble factor A; Nobel 1929 to Eijkman and Hopkins.

| | **Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K** | **Water-soluble: B-complex, C** |
|---|---|---|
| Absorption | with dietary fat and bile | directly into blood |
| Storage | stored (liver, fat) | not stored (except **B12** in the liver) |
| Excess | can be **toxic** (A, D) | excreted in urine; rarely toxic |
| Daily need | not essential every day | needed regularly |
| Cooking loss | fairly stable | lost in cooking water; **C** destroyed by heat and air |

- **Fat-soluble**: vitamins **A and D** (of A, B, C, D). [[PYQ: YOGA-2017NOV-P2-Q17]]
- **Water-soluble**: vitamins **B and C**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2019DEC-P2-Q109]]

## 2. Fat-soluble vitamins

| Vitamin | Chemical name | Functions | Sources | Deficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **retinol** (provitamin: **β-carotene**) | **vision** (rhodopsin in rods), epithelia, immunity, growth | liver, fish-liver oil, egg yolk, milk; **carrots, green leafy vegetables, mango, papaya, pumpkin** (as β-carotene) | **night blindness** (earliest), conjunctival xerosis, **Bitot's spots**, **xerophthalmia**, **keratomalacia** (corneal melting) → blindness |
| **D** | **calciferol** (D2 ergocalciferol, D3 cholecalciferol); active form **calcitriol** (1,25-dihydroxy-D3, made in the kidney) | **absorption of calcium and phosphorus** from the gut; bone mineralisation | **sunlight** (UV-B on skin), fish, egg yolk, fortified milk | **rickets** in children; **osteomalacia** in adults |
| **E** | **tocopherol** | **antioxidant**: protects cell membranes and PUFA from oxidation | vegetable oils, nuts, seeds, wheat germ | haemolytic anaemia (premature babies), neuropathy; infertility in rats ("anti-sterility vitamin") |
| **K** | phylloquinone (K1, plants), menaquinone (K2, gut bacteria) | **blood clotting** (factors II, VII, IX, X); bone proteins | green leafy vegetables, cabbage; gut bacteria | bleeding; haemorrhagic disease of the newborn (hence vitamin K injection at birth) |

**Vitamin A:**
- The provitamin converted into vitamin A in the body is **β-carotene** (one molecule → two retinol in theory). [[PYQ: YOGA-2025JUN-P2-Q113]]
- India's **vitamin A prophylaxis** [[LIKELY]]: 1 lakh IU at 9 months, then 2 lakh IU every 6 months up to 5 years.

**Vitamin D:**
- In young children its deficiency causes **rickets** (soft, bent bones: bow legs, knock knees, **rickety rosary**, pigeon chest, delayed fontanelle closure). [[PYQ: YOGA-2017NOV-P3-Q13]]
- In adults it causes **osteomalacia** (bone softening, pain). [[PYQ: YOGA-2019DEC-P2-Q089]] (Osteoporosis — loss of bone mass — is a different disease, mainly of ageing and oestrogen loss.)
- Rickets in childhood is due to **hypocalcaemia** (low calcium and phosphate, from vitamin D lack). [[PYQ: YOGA-2024JUN-P2-Q093]]

**Matching asked**: **vitamin A → good vision; vitamin D → strong bones; vitamin K → blood clotting; iron → haemoglobin formation**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017NOV-P3-Q67]] And: **D → absorption of calcium and phosphorus; E → protection of cells (antioxidant); B6 → energy transformation in brain and nervous tissue; K → clotting and coagulation of blood**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2022JUN-P2-Q122]]

## 3. Water-soluble vitamins

| Vitamin | Name | Coenzyme / function | Sources | Deficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **thiamine** | TPP: carbohydrate metabolism, nerve function | whole grains, unpolished/parboiled rice, pulses, nuts, yeast | **beriberi** — dry (**polyneuritis**, peripheral neuropathy), wet (heart failure, oedema), infantile; **Wernicke–Korsakoff** syndrome in alcoholics |
| **B2** | **riboflavin** | FAD, FMN: energy release | **milk**, eggs, liver, green leafy vegetables | **angular stomatitis** (cracks at mouth corners), cheilosis, **glossitis** (magenta tongue), corneal vascularisation |
| **B3** | **niacin** (nicotinic acid) | NAD, NADP | meat, fish, groundnut, pulses; made from **tryptophan** (60 mg → 1 mg) | **pellagra**: the "3 Ds" — **diarrhoea, dermatitis** (sun-exposed skin, Casal's necklace), **dementia** (+ death); common among **maize and jowar** eaters |
| B5 | pantothenic acid | coenzyme A | widespread | "burning feet" syndrome |
| **B6** | **pyridoxine** | PLP: amino-acid metabolism; makes **neurotransmitters** (serotonin, GABA, dopamine) | cereals, pulses, meat, banana | peripheral neuritis, **convulsions**, microcytic anaemia (risk with the TB drug isoniazid) |
| B7 | **biotin** | carboxylation reactions | liver, egg yolk, nuts; gut bacteria | **dermatitis**, hair loss (raw egg white's avidin binds biotin) |
| **B9** | **folic acid** (folate) | DNA synthesis, cell division | green leafy vegetables, pulses, liver | **megaloblastic anaemia**; **neural tube defects** in the fetus (hence folic acid before and in early pregnancy) |
| **B12** | **cyanocobalamin** (contains **cobalt**) | DNA synthesis; myelin | **only animal foods**: meat, fish, eggs, milk | **pernicious / megaloblastic anaemia**; **subacute combined degeneration** of the spinal cord; vegetarians and vegans at risk |
| **C** | **ascorbic acid** | **collagen** formation, antioxidant, **iron absorption**, wound healing, immunity | **amla, guava**, citrus, tomato, green leafy vegetables, sprouts | **scurvy**: bleeding, spongy gums, **subcutaneous / perifollicular bleeding**, poor wound healing, bone pain |

**B-vitamin matching asked:**
- **B1 → beriberi; B2 → angular stomatitis; B12 → megaloblastic anaemia; B3 → pellagra.** [[PYQ: YOGA-2018JUL-P2-X05]]
- **Thiamine → beriberi; niacin → pellagra; biotin → dermatitis; cyanocobalamin → pernicious anaemia.** [[PYQ: YOGA-2024SEP-P2-Q113]]
- **Niacin → diarrhoea, dementia and dermatitis; ascorbic acid → subcutaneous bleeding; magnesium → tetany; thiamine → polyneuritis.** [[PYQ: YOGA-2024DEC-P2-Q132]]
- **Vitamin B (niacin) → pellagra; C → scurvy; D → rickets; E → (general) malnutrition.** [[PYQ: YOGA-2021JUN-P2-S01]]
- **Riboflavin** deficiency causes **angular stomatitis**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2020JUN-P2-Q085]]

**Vitamin B12:**
- **Pernicious anaemia** is due to deficiency of **cyanocobalamin** — specifically an autoimmune loss of **intrinsic factor** (from the stomach's parietal cells), so B12 cannot be absorbed in the terminal ileum. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P2-Q12]] (Minot and Murphy treated it with liver, 1926; Nobel 1934.)
- B12 is essential for **DNA synthesis in RBCs**; its deficiency stops cells maturing and reduces cell division, so large immature RBCs (megaloblasts) appear. [[PYQ: YOGA-2021JUN-P2-S53]]

**Vitamin C:**
- Main sources: **fresh fruits and green leafy vegetables** (liver and egg yolk have little). [[PYQ: YOGA-2019JUN-P2-Q74]]
- Content (mg/100 g, Indian food tables): **amla ~600 > guava ~212 > orange ~30–50 > tomato ~27**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P3-Q59]]

[[LIKELY]] Folic acid and neural tube defects; B6 and convulsions; biotin and raw egg; vitamin C and iron absorption; vitamin K at birth; which vitamin contains cobalt.

## 4. Minerals

**Macro-minerals** (need > 100 mg/day): calcium, phosphorus, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chloride, sulphur. **Trace elements** (micro-minerals): iron, iodine, zinc, copper, fluoride, selenium, manganese, chromium, molybdenum, cobalt.

| Mineral | Functions | Sources | Deficiency / excess |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calcium** | bones and teeth (99%), muscle contraction, nerve conduction, clotting, heart rhythm | **ragi** (~344–364 mg/100 g — the richest cereal), **sesame (til)** (> 1,000 mg), milk and curd (~120 mg/100 mL), green leafy vegetables, small fish | rickets, osteomalacia, **osteoporosis**, **tetany** (low blood Ca²⁺) |
| Phosphorus | bones, ATP, DNA, buffers | milk, cereals, pulses, meat | rare |
| **Magnesium** | cofactor for 300+ enzymes (ATP), nerve and muscle function | whole grains, nuts, green leafy vegetables | muscle cramps, **tetany**, arrhythmia |
| Sodium / potassium | fluid balance, nerve impulses, BP | salt / fruits (banana), vegetables, coconut water | hyponatraemia / hypokalaemia; excess salt → hypertension (WHO: < 5 g salt a day) |
| **Iron** | **haemoglobin** and myoglobin, enzymes | haem iron (meat, fish: better absorbed); non-haem (green leafy vegetables, **garden cress seeds**, jaggery, dates, pulses, millets) | **iron-deficiency anaemia** (microcytic, hypochromic): India's most common nutritional deficiency (NFHS-5: ~57% of women anaemic) |
| **Iodine** | **thyroid hormones** (T3, T4) | iodised salt, sea food | **goitre**, **cretinism**, iodine-deficiency disorders; iodised salt (30 ppm at production, 15 ppm at consumer level; national programme NIDDCP) |
| **Zinc** | 300+ enzymes, immunity, wound healing, growth, taste | meat, pulses, nuts, whole grains | growth retardation, delayed sexual maturity, poor immunity, diarrhoea |
| **Copper** | enzymes: **tyrosinase** (makes **melanin** — natural **colour of skin and hair**), lysyl oxidase (collagen), cytochrome oxidase, ceruloplasmin (iron metabolism); **oxidation of fatty acids and vitamin C** | nuts, seeds, liver, whole grains | anaemia, depigmentation; Menkes disease; excess: **Wilson's disease** |
| **Fluoride** | hardens tooth enamel | water (~1 ppm), tea | dental caries / excess: **fluorosis** (mottled teeth, skeletal) |
| **Selenium** | antioxidant enzyme glutathione peroxidase | sea food, cereals | Keshan disease (cardiomyopathy) |

- The **richest food source of calcium** among the options was **ragi**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2017JAN-P3-Q16]]
- The functions of **copper**: **natural colour of skin and hair** and **oxidation of fatty acids and vitamin C**. [[PYQ: YOGA-2023JUN-P2-Q42]]

**Iron absorption** [[LIKELY]] — increased by **vitamin C** and haem iron; decreased by **tea and coffee** (tannins), **phytates** (bran), oxalates, and calcium taken together. National Iron Plus Initiative / **Anaemia Mukt Bharat** (2018): weekly or daily iron–folic acid tablets.

## 5. Antioxidants, anti-inflammatory nutrients and phytochemicals

**Free radicals** — unstable molecules with an unpaired electron (superoxide, hydroxyl radical) produced in metabolism, smoking, pollution, stress; they damage DNA, proteins and lipids (**oxidative stress**) and contribute to ageing, heart disease, cancer and diabetes.

**Antioxidants** neutralise them: **vitamins C, E, A (β-carotene)**, **selenium**, **zinc**, **copper** and **manganese** (in antioxidant enzymes: superoxide dismutase, catalase, glutathione peroxidase), and plant **polyphenols** (flavonoids such as **quercetin**, catechins in green tea, **curcumin** in turmeric, resveratrol). Research on yoga reports reduced markers of oxidative stress. [[LIKELY]]

**Anti-inflammatory nutrients** include **quercetin**, **B-complex vitamins** and **omega-3 fatty acids** (also curcumin, ginger, vitamin D). [[PYQ: YOGA-2024DEC-P2-Q119]]

**Phytochemicals** — non-nutrient plant compounds with health benefits; "eat a rainbow":

| Colour | Phytochemical | Foods |
|---|---|---|
| **Orange / yellow** | **β-carotene** (carotenoids) | carrot, pumpkin, papaya, mango, sweet potato |
| **Red** | **lycopene** | tomato, watermelon, pink guava |
| **Deep red / red-purple** | **betalains** | beetroot |
| **Blue / purple** | **anthocyanins** | brinjal skin, jamun, black grapes, purple cabbage |
| Green | chlorophyll, lutein, **glucosinolates / sulforaphane** (cruciferous) | spinach, broccoli, cabbage |
| White | **allicin** (garlic), quercetin (onion) | garlic, onion, cauliflower |
